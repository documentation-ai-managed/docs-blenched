## Overview

Easily connect your Google account for access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit login page">
    Go to `https://blenched.com/login`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google sign-in">
    Click the "Sign in with Google" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Approve the permissions requested. That's it—you're connected!
  </Step>
</Steps>
