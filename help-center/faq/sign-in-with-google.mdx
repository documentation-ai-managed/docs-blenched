## Overview

Blenched uses Google for secure sign-in.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://blenched.com/login`
2. Click "Sign in with Google"
3. Select your Google account
4. Confirm any permissions

## Why Google?

- No extra passwords
- Quick, secure authentication
