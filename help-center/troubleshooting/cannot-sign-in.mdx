description: Resolve login problems with Google sign-in.
---

## Problem

- Sign-in fails or page reloads
- Error: "Could not authenticate"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Google account">
    Ensure you're logged into your Google account on the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear cache">
    ````
    Clear browser cache and cookies, then reload.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try incognito mode">
    Some plugins may block sign-in. Try using incognito/private browsing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
